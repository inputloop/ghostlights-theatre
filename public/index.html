Ghostlights

A ghost light is the one lamp left burning on an empty stage, so whoever comes in first can find their way.

Here, a ghostlight is a show made from one piece of writing. Each claim in it names its witness: the file it came from, when it was read, and a hash of what was read, so a reader can go and check.

Now showing

To the Two Readers A mark from Corwin's hand: the line is written, TRUE is struck, "two" comes in another ink, and the page turns to the reader across the table. Read with the paper it answers, every line with its witness.
